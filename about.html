Our Story

Good ol' southern hospitality

Housed in a beautifully restored 1850s historic home, our space blends deep roots with a welcoming, casual atmosphere. Inside, you’ll find cozy, original dark wood paneling and a crackling fireplace at the bar—perfect for crisp evenings. Upstairs, our secondary dining room offers a relaxed escape from the hustle and bustle below. But the true heart of Michael’s lies just beyond the back doors: our spacious, dog-friendly outdoor patios. Framed by historic brick and open skies, it’s the ultimate neighborhood gathering spot to kick back with friends, listen to live local music, or challenge your mind during our high-energy, weekly DJ trivia nights.

Our menu focuses on elevated, hearty American pub classics and comforting tavern favorites, highlighting wild-caught Gulf shrimp as a nod to our dedication to coastal flavor and supporting local fishing communities wherever possible. From crisp Chicken Wings and towering plates of fresh Pub Nachos to savory Burgers and fresh seafood handhelds, every dish is crafted to pair perfectly with a cold drink. Our full bar features Thursday Draft Beer specials with 12 taps to choose from, Daily classic cocktail features, and our signature scratch-made sour mix.

At Michael’s, we believe in genuine hospitality, cold drinks, and a space where every regular and first-time visitor feels right at home. Pull up a chair inside by the fire, or stretch out on the patio with your favorite four-legged companion—we’ve got a seat saved for you.
