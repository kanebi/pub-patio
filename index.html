Our story

Michael's opened in a converted early-1900s home at 208 Pollock St, downtown New Bern — tucked between the municipal buildings and the boat docks. We kept the porches, the courtyard, and the creaky charm, then filled it with a full bar, an upstairs dining room, and two dog-friendly patios. Jerk-spiced pub fare, jumbo wings, and prices that keep locals coming back.

FAQs

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Both patios and the courtyard welcome dogs, with water bowls and a dog menu on request.

Do you take reservations or big groups?

Yes — the upstairs dining room seats large parties. Call (252) 631-1209 about hosting a celebration.

Is there takeout and delivery?

Dine-in, takeout, and delivery. Call the pub or ask your server about the current draft list and daily catch.

Where do I park?

Street parking and a nearby public lot. We're at 208 Pollock St, a short walk from Union Point and the waterfront.

What are your hours?

Mon–Tue 11–9, Wed–Thu 11–10, Fri–Sat 11–11, Sunday 12–8:30.
